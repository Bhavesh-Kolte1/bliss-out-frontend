Privacy Policy

Your privacy and trust are important to us. This page explains how Bliss Out Dance Studio collects, uses, stores and protects your information, along with our refund, cancellation and digital delivery policies.

🗒 Effective Date: 4 August 2026
🛡

Privacy Policy

At Bliss Out Dance Studio, protecting the trust of every dancer who joins us matters as much as the choreography itself. This section explains what information we collect, why we collect it, and how it is kept safe.

Information We Collect

When you register for a workshop or reach out to us, we may collect the following details:

  • ✦ Full Name
  • ✦ Email Address
  • ✦ Phone Number
  • ✦ Age
  • ✦ Dance Level
  • ✦ City
  • ✦ Payment details, processed securely via Razorpay
🔒 Your card, UPI and banking details are never stored on our servers. All payments are handled directly and securely by Razorpay's payment infrastructure.

How We Use Your Information

The information you share with us is used only to serve you better, specifically to:

  • ✦ Process and confirm your Garba workshop registration
  • ✦ Verify successful payment
  • ✦ Generate and deliver your digital Garba Pass
  • ✦ Communicate updates and reminders via WhatsApp
  • ✦ Communicate confirmations and updates via Email
  • ✦ Provide customer support when you reach out to us
  • ✦ Manage batches, schedules and overall workshop logistics

Data Security

We follow reasonable, industry-appropriate security measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, alteration or disclosure. While no method of transmission over the internet is completely infallible, we take your privacy seriously and work to safeguard it at every step. Your personal data is never sold, rented or traded to any third party.

Third-Party Services

To operate smoothly, Bliss Out Dance Studio relies on a small number of trusted third-party services, including:

  • ✦ Razorpay — for secure payment processing
  • ✦ Email service providers — for confirmations and updates
  • ✦ WhatsApp Business Platform — for pass delivery and communication

Each of these services operates under its own privacy policy, and we encourage you to review them to understand how they handle your data.

↺

Refund & Cancellation Policy

  • ✦ Registration fees are generally non-refundable once payment has been successfully completed.
  • ✦ In the case of duplicate payments, the extra amount will be refunded after verification.
  • ✦ If Bliss Out Dance Studio cancels a workshop for any reason, all eligible participants will receive a full refund.
  • ✦ If your payment succeeds but registration fails due to a technical issue, please contact us with your payment details. Once verified, we will provide an appropriate resolution, including a refund where applicable.
📦

Shipping & Delivery Policy

Bliss Out Dance Studio sells only digital workshop registrations. No physical products are shipped or delivered.

What You Receive

Once your payment has been verified, you will receive:

  • ✦ Registration Confirmation
  • ✦ Garba Pass (PDF)
  • ✦ Email Confirmation
  • ✦ WhatsApp Pass (when available)

Delivery usually happens within a few minutes of successful payment. If you haven't received your confirmation, please reach out to the studio and we'll help sort it out right away.

Terms & Conditions

These Terms & Conditions govern your use of this website and your registration for Bliss Out Dance Studio's Garba workshops. By registering for a workshop, you agree to the terms explained below.

1. Acceptance of Terms

By registering for a workshop or using this website, you agree to these Terms & Conditions. Please read them carefully before proceeding with your registration.

2. Workshop Registration

Workshop registration is done online through this website. You are responsible for providing accurate and complete details, such as your name, contact number and email address, at the time of registration.

3. Eligibility

Our Garba workshops are open to all age groups, subject to any age requirement mentioned for a particular batch. Participants below 18 years of age should register with the consent of a parent or guardian.

4. Payments

All workshop payments are made online through Razorpay. A seat is not held or reserved for you until the payment is completed successfully.

5. Registration Confirmation

Your registration is confirmed only after your payment is successfully received and verified. Submitting the registration form alone, without completing payment, does not guarantee you a seat.

6. Batch Capacity

Seats in every batch are limited. Once a batch reaches full capacity, registrations for that batch will be closed, even if you have not yet completed your payment.

7. Student Responsibilities

Students are expected to arrive on time, come prepared for practice, and provide accurate registration details. You are responsible for keeping your registration confirmation and Garba Pass safe for entry.

8. Code of Conduct

  • ✦ Students must behave respectfully with instructors, staff and fellow participants at all times.
  • ✦ The studio may refuse or cancel a registration in case of misconduct, without any refund.

9. Attendance

Students are encouraged to attend all sessions of their batch regularly to get the full benefit of the workshop. Missing a session does not entitle a student to an extra class or a refund.

10. Cancellation by Studio

Workshop timings may occasionally change due to unavoidable circumstances such as venue availability, weather or instructor availability. In such cases, Bliss Out Dance Studio will inform registered students in advance, wherever possible.

11. Intellectual Property

All choreography, dance sequences and training material taught during the workshop belong to Bliss Out Dance Studio. They should not be recorded and taught commercially without our written permission.

12. Limitation of Liability

Students must follow their instructor's guidance during all sessions. Bliss Out Dance Studio is not liable for injuries caused by not following safety instructions or instructor guidance during the workshop.

13. Privacy

Any personal information you share with us during registration is collected, used and protected as explained in the Privacy Policy above.

14. Governing Law

These Terms & Conditions are governed by the laws of India. Any disputes arising from them will be subject to the jurisdiction of the courts in Khandwa, Madhya Pradesh.
