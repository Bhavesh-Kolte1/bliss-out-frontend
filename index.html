What You'll Learn

A Complete Journey From Zero to Garba

From your very first step to becoming a confident performer, our workshop is thoughtfully designed to be fun, progressive, and performance-focused.

𝄞
Rhythm & Musicality

Develop a strong sense of rhythm by mastering Garba's 4-count, 6-count, and 8-count patterns. Learn how to hear the music, understand timing, and dance in perfect sync.

𓀥
Footwork & Technique

Master everything from beginner to advance footwork, from Chakadhi, Dodhiya, 3-Talli to Spins, Barrels, Raas patterns, and choreography sections.

˙ᵕ˙
Expression & Mudras

Learn hand gestures and facial expressions that bring Garba alive with emotion and grace.

𓏵
Dandiya Raas

Explore traditional and modern Dandiya techniques, group formations, and safe dandiya-work for Navratri and stage performances.

👁
Performance Prepration

Build stage confidence with choreography, formations, performance etiquette, rehearsal techniques, and presentation skills that prepare you for live performances.
